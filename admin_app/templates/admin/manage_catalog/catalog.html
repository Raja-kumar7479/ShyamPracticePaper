This screen controls the whole platform's vocabulary

A group is an exam family (GATE, Placement) and is managed under Courses & Groups. Each group offers a set of sections. A section is a kind of content, and its portal decides which screen a student lands on when they open it. Changes take effect immediately - on this app at once, and on the student app within a minute.
